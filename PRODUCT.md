# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are Tier-1 industrial, civil engineering, mining, and infrastructure project managers, safety officers, and procurement directors in South Africa. They operate in high-risk, high-stakes environments where project delays, equipment failure, or safety incidents carry catastrophic costs. They require certainty, proven engineering competency, and specialized heavy lifting equipment.

Secondary audiences include commercial builders, plant maintenance coordinators, and industrial logistics managers seeking reliable mobile crane hire, rigging crews, and abnormal transport services.

## Product Purpose

Serve as the digital flagship and operational procurement portal for Teemane Cranes. The site validates company credibility, showcases heavy fleet capabilities (up to 440T) and landmark projects, and funnels qualified engineering inquiries directly into site inspections, lift studies, and hire bookings.

## Positioning

Turnkey engineered lifting solutions backed by in-house CAD lift studies, comprehensive risk assessments, and a heavy crane fleet up to 440T. Unlike standard dry/wet equipment rental brokers, Teemane Cranes delivers total engineering certainty, hands-on site management, and uncompromising zero-incident safety on South Africa's most complex worksites.

## Operating Context

- Industrial worksites across South Africa, including mining facilities, energy plants, petrochemical refineries, marine ports, and civil structural projects.
- Operations demand strict regulatory OHS compliance, formal method statements, CAD rigging drawings, and rigorous site pre-inspections before equipment mobilization.
- Decision-makers frequently evaluate technical load charts, boom reaches, transport logistics, and project portfolio proof before initiating formal tender requests or 24/7 emergency calls.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fleet capabilities: Mobile hydraulic and all-terrain cranes up to 440 tons capacity.
- Service offerings: Short-term and long-term crane hire, specialized rigging, abnormal load transport, CAD lift planning, and 24/7 emergency response.
- Technical stack: Laravel 13, Inertia.js v3, React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, Vite, Leaflet, and accessible interactive galleries.
- Undecided facts: Exact dynamic rate card and live GPS fleet telematics remain internal backend assets and are not exposed directly to public web visitors without consultation.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Teemane Cranes
- Core Taglines: "A Cut Above The Rest" and "Redefining What's Possible In Mobile Crane Hire."
- Heritage: Established in 2012, backed by 100+ years of combined management leadership and 500+ completed projects.
- Tone of Voice: Authoritative, disciplined, technically precise, and dependable. No superficial hype or generic contractor jargon.
- Visual Identity: Industrial authority featuring deep navy/royal blue tones, crisp high-contrast utility backgrounds, and industrial precision details.

## Evidence on Hand

- Verified project archives and on-site photography in `data-docs/` and public assets (e.g., landmark Media 24 structural lifts, marine vessel operations, multi-crane tandem lifts).
- Confirmed operational benchmarks: Established 2012, 440T maximum lift capacity, 100+ years combined management experience, 500+ successful projects, 24/7 emergency response.
- Ground rule: Never fabricate unverified partner certifications, artificial client reviews, or fictitious fleet models.

## Product Principles

1. **Certainty Before Execution**: Showcase engineering discipline—CAD lift studies, thorough inspections, and certified rigging expertise precede every crane mobilization.
2. **Authentic Heavy Equipment Proof**: Real job-site photography, accurate load capacities, and verified technical specifications always triumph over generic stock images.
3. **Direct Procurement Pathway**: Make evaluating crane availability, reviewing fleet specs, and requesting emergency assistance or lift consultations seamless on both desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Adhere to WCAG 2.1 AA standards across contrast ratios, clear text hierarchy, and semantic markup.
- Ensure all interactive fleet specs, thumbnail switchers, and full-screen lightbox galleries are keyboard-navigable and screen-reader accessible.
